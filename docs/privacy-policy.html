Sudoku

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective June 2026

Short version Sudoku collects no personal data, connects to no servers, and shares nothing with anyone. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

Sudoku is developed and maintained by Radu Slăvilă (the "Developer"). This policy describes what information the app handles, how it is stored, and what your rights are.

The app is designed to function entirely offline. It requires no account, no login, and no network connection to play or use any of its features.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Specifically, Sudoku does not collect your name, email address, device identifier, IP address, location, usage analytics, crash diagnostics, or any other information that could identify you as an individual.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally in your device's browser storage (IndexedDB), solely to provide its core functionality:

  • The current in-progress game state (board, timer, move history)
  • Completed game records used to calculate statistics (difficulty, time, outcome)
  • Your preferences (theme, per-difficulty hint and mistake limits)

This data never leaves your device. It is not synced to any server, not accessible by the Developer, and not associated with your identity in any way. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

Third parties

Sudoku does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, social login, crash reporting services, or tracking libraries. No data is shared with any third party.

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google may collect information about your device and app usage in accordance with their own privacy policy, over which the Developer has no control.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, files, or any other sensitive device permission. No special Android permissions beyond basic app operation are required or requested.

Children's privacy

Sudoku does not knowingly collect any information from children or from anyone. Because no data collection occurs at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Open source

Sudoku is free and open-source software, licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0. The complete source code is publicly available for inspection on GitHub.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated in a meaningful way (for example, if a future version of the app introduces optional cloud saves or analytics), the effective date above will be updated and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
